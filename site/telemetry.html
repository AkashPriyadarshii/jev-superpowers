Cost Efficiency

Token Economics: $0.042 / Million Tokens

Generative reasoning loops cost $3.00 to $15.00 per million tokens. System One typed decisions cost pennies per day of continuous coding.

Because Jev decisions emit zero conversational tokens, a full day of 400 gated agent actions consumes less than 80,000 tokens total, costing approximately $0.0034 per developer-day.
